Explore knowledge maps and turn practice into an adventure.

**Grade Saga** is a gamified multi-disciplinary practice app. Transforming fundamental subject concepts—Math, Physics, Chemistry, English, and more—into an interconnected quest map and level-based challenges, Grade Saga helps learners move beyond monotonous drills, sparking genuine learning interest and building solid academic foundations through progressive stage-clearing.

Turn rote drills into interactive map exploration and quest challenges! Grade Saga covers key concepts across math, physics, chemistry, and English. With gradual level progression and instant feedback, learners can easily build study habits and reinforce subject essentials in an engaging, milestone-driven journey.

## Key Features

- **Exploratory Knowledge Maps**: Organizes subject topics into intuitive stage maps. From core definitions to comprehensive problem-solving, your learning path is clearly visualized with defined goals.
- **Quest & Stage-Clearing Experience**: Each level has clear passing requirements. Meet the benchmark to light up the node and unlock subsequent challenges, replacing aimless quiz anxiety with purpose and achievement.
- **Multi-Subject Core Practice**: Covers fundamental practice across mathematics, physics, chemistry, English, and more. Systematically broken down by knowledge modules for flexible, targeted review anytime.
- **Instant Feedback & Concept Breakdown**: Review your answers and mapped knowledge points immediately upon submission, helping learners pinpoint weak spots and master the core concepts behind every question.
- **Stress-Free Replay**: Revisit unlocked levels anytime to beat your high score. Strengthen mastery through spaced review and foster confident, self-driven study habits.
- **Clean & Focused Experience**: Clean, distraction-free interface with offline practice support—start a focused practice session anytime, anywhere.

## Who It's For

- Learners looking to spark curiosity and build consistent daily study habits
- Self-studiers seeking to reinforce foundational math, science, and English skills and target gaps
- Anyone who loves step-by-step progress, gamified challenges, and instant feedback
