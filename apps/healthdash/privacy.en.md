My Health Dash ("we", "our", or "the App") is built with a local-first privacy approach. Your personal and family health records stay on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how My Health Dash handles your information.

---

## 1. Local Data Storage

All core data you enter into My Health Dash is stored **locally on your device**:

- **Profiles:** Display name, biological sex, birth date, and optional blood type.
- **Health Records:** Biomarker values, measurement dates, units, notes, and checkup report details.
- **AI Insights History:** Saved AI summaries stored on your device.

We do **not** require account registration, and we do **not** operate a cloud database that stores or syncs your health records.

---

## 2. Optional De-Identified AI Health Insights

My Health Dash includes an **optional** AI Health Insights feature that runs only when you explicitly tap to request an analysis. Otherwise, no health data leaves your device.

When you request an analysis:

1. **De-Identification:** Before sending any request, personal identifiers such as your display name, exact birth date, facility names, and personal notes are removed. Only your age in whole years, biological sex, optional blood type, selected biomarker values/units/dates/reference ranges, and display language are sent.
2. **Encrypted Transit:** The de-identified request is sent over an encrypted connection to generate your summary in real time. Requests are not linked to any user account, email address, phone number, or advertising identifier, and generated summaries are saved only on your device.

If you prefer not to transmit any data off your device, simply do not use the AI Health Insights feature; all other tracking, charting, and backup features work completely offline.

---

## 3. Backups & Exports

My Health Dash provides user-initiated backup and spreadsheet export tools:

- Export files are created locally and saved to the location you choose via your device's system share sheet or file picker.
- If you choose to save a backup to Apple iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another third-party storage provider, that file is governed by that provider's privacy policy.
- Backup files contain your health records so they can be restored across devices. Please store them in a trusted, password-protected location.

---

## 4. No Ads, No Tracking, No Data Selling

- My Health Dash contains no third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.
- We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete any measurement, report, AI summary, or profile inside the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device (any backup files you manually exported must be deleted separately by you).

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

My Health Dash supports multi-profile family tracking (for example, a parent recording a child's checkups on the parent's own device). The App is not directed to children for independent use, and we do not collect personal information from children on any server.
