## Intro

Focus Brief is an app that helps you stay focused on the information that matters.

Step away from the daily flood of information and irrelevant distractions, and build your own focus center.

The interface is simple and clean.
No sign-up or login required. Open it and start using it right away.
Your personal data stays only on your device and is never uploaded to the cloud, protecting your privacy.

You can choose a preset topic or create your own focus topics and information sources. Focus Brief gathers the latest content from multiple sources, filters it, removes duplicates, organizes it, and presents it as concise summaries to reduce unnecessary reading.

Key features:

* Organize the latest information by topic
* Support preset and custom focus topics
* Support multiple public information sources
* Manual refresh and scheduled reminders
* Save important content
* Keep original titles, sources, and links
* Support language, region, and display settings
